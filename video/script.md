# Sentinel demo video: script

Under 3 minutes (the rules say judges may stop watching at 3:00), read by en-GB-SoniaNeural.

How to mark the narration (`video/voice.py` reads these marks):

- `/` is a short pause, like a breath.
- `//` is a longer pause, to let a point land.
- `*words*` stresses those words: they're read a little slower and louder.

Only the `>` lines are spoken. Everything else is notes for the picture.

## 1. Hook

**On screen:** the lobby, with seven shoppers standing in a circle. Slow zoom in.

> Your checkout isn't broken… / it's just broken for _someone you've never met._ // This is Sentinel.

## 2. The problem

**On screen:** the slide "Shops are built on fast laptops…", or the store home page with a caption.

> Most shops get built on fast laptops, by people with steady hands and good eyes. / And the shoppers who don't fit that don't complain. / They just _leave._ // The owner sees a lower number, / and _never finds out why._

## 3. Meet the shoppers

**On screen:** "Start your trial". The circle walks into a line. Iris and Tobin get picked.

> So Sentinel sends in seven little pixel shoppers to try and buy from you. // Grandma Iris can't see too well, / Tobin's hands shake, / Pell's stuck on a slow connection, / Knock only uses a keyboard, / Echo listens with a screen reader, / Dash is on a cheap phone, / and Cog's an AI agent that shops all on its own.

## 4. Iris walks the checkout

**On screen:** Iris walks the store. Zoom on the faint "Full name" field, with the background blurred. The number 1.6 counts up.

> They walk a real checkout on our demo store, / and everything they find is measured right there, on the live page. // Here, Iris hits a field she just _can't read._ / The contrast is one point six to one, / when it needs at least four point five. // Every problem costs her coins and patience, / and once that runs out? / _So does the sale._

## 5. Tobin's shaky taps

**On screen:** Tobin at the cart. Zoom on the red and green tap marks.

> Tobin taps every button twenty times, the way shaky hands really would. / The red marks are the ones that missed, / and a tiny remove link sitting right next to checkout? / That's _a disaster waiting to happen._

## 6. Results

**On screen:** "See the full results". The Summary tab. Zoom on "money at risk" as the number counts up.

> When they're done, you get the results in plain words. / Who bought, who gave up, and where, / plus a rough idea of _what it's costing you_ each month. // And Gemini writes the verdict from the actual measurements, / not guesswork.

## 7. Fix and run again

**On screen:** the Problems and fixes tab, with the code for a fix. Click "Try the fixes and run the trial again". Iris comes back, and the "Fixed" entries light up.

> Every problem comes with a fix you can try right on the page, in one click, / then you just send the shoppers back in. // Iris goes from nine problems and a lost sale / to _none at all,_ / and this time she _actually pays,_ through the Braintree sandbox.

## 8. Cog, the AI agent

**On screen:** a Cog trial. Zoom on the order being placed.

> Cog's a little different. / It's an AI agent that shops by itself, / so it reads the product data, decides whether it's worth buying, / and places a real sandbox order, with every price checked on the server.

## 9. Ledger and your own site

**On screen:** the ledger. Then the "Try it on your own site" page with its one-line script.

> Everything ends up in the ledger, where you can sort it, filter it, and see what's fixed. // And on your own site, / you just paste in _one line,_ and the shoppers can come and visit. / There, they only look around. / They never buy.

## 10. Where it's going

**On screen:** the deck slide "Where it's going". Slow zoom in.

> Today, it's checkouts. / Next, it's whole websites and apps, / more kinds of people, / and security checks too. // As AI gets better at simulating people, / it'll need to simulate _their limits_ as well.

## 11. Close

**On screen:** the last slide, with the link.

> Sentinel. // Meet the customers _your checkout's been losing._
