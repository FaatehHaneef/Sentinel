The Checkout Trials

Sentinel

Your checkout isn't broken.
It's just broken for someone you've never met.

Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon · Braintree sandbox · Gemini

The problem

Shops are built on fast laptops by people with steady hands and good eyes.

Low vision

A shopper with low vision can't read pale text.

Slow signal

A shopper on one bar of signal gives up on a heavy page.

No mouse

A shopper without a mouse can't reach a fake button.

They leave quietly. The shop owner only ever sees a lower sales number, never why.

The idea

Don't read a report. Watch them try to buy from you.

Sentinel sends seven pixel-art shoppers through a real checkout. Each one stands for a real kind of customer, checks the page the way that customer would, and reacts when something gets in the way.

Every problem costs coins from their purse. Lose too much patience, lose the sale.

Meet the shoppers

Seven kinds of customer. 29 checks between them.

How it works

PayPal and AI do real work.

Braintree sandbox

Real orders with line items, prices set on the server, declines, double-submit protection, and verified webhooks.

Gemini

Writes each verdict from the measurements. Cog's agent decides whether to buy. Suggested colours must pass the contrast maths.

Safe by design

Runs only on our demo store or sites whose owner added one line. Sandbox only. On owners' sites, shoppers only look.
