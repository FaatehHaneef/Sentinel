Where PayPal and AI do the work

Braintree sandbox (PayPal)

The demo store's real checkout: line items with prices set on the server, protection against double payment, handled declines, and signature-checked webhooks.

Gemini

Writes each shopper's verdict from the measurements, suggests colours that must pass the contrast maths, and runs Cog's buying agent. Every number is the browser's, never the AI's.

Things you might notice

🤖
"Wick's standard notes, written without AI."

Gemini runs on its free tier, which has a daily limit. When it's used up or Google is busy, Sentinel uses its own notes and says so, and Cog uses the ordinary checkout. Try again later for Gemini's version.

💳
No PayPal button in the payment form.

PayPal accounts, including developer sandbox ones, aren't available to developers in Pakistan, where Sentinel was built. So payments use Braintree's sandbox with cards. The PayPal button is wired in and turns on with one setting once an account is linked.

📒
The ledger only shows your trials.

There are no accounts yet, so records stay in your own browser.

Safe by design

  • Runs only on our demo store, or on sites whose owner added Sentinel's line of code. There, the shoppers only look: they never type, pay or buy.
  • Sandbox only: a live payments setting makes the app refuse to start.
  • Only fixes from a fixed allow-list can ever be applied to a page.
